# Review Rules

Reusable guidance for reviewing code, UX, accessibility, and implementation quality.

## Review Focus

- Prioritize correctness, regressions, accessibility, maintainability, and missing verification.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions.
- Identify the exact file, element, behavior, or flow when reporting an issue.
- Rank findings by impact and likelihood.

## Review Output

- Lead with actionable findings.
- Include severity, evidence, user or system impact, and a recommended fix.
- State clearly when no issues are found.
- List verification gaps and residual risk.
