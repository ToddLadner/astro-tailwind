# General Rules

Reusable development behavior for any coding or UX project.

## Working Standards

- Inspect relevant files before proposing or making changes.
- Do not invent dependencies, frameworks, APIs, files, or project conventions.
- Prefer the smallest complete change that satisfies the task.
- Match existing naming, formatting, structure, and patterns.
- Preserve established behavior, architecture, and public APIs unless the task requires a change.
- Surface assumptions and uncertainty explicitly.
- Keep responses concise and direct.

## Boundaries

- Do not edit unrelated files.
- Do not remove comments, tests, accessibility behavior, or error handling without explaining why.
- Never claim validation passed unless it was run successfully.
