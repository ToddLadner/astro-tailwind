# Produce a Verifiable Handoff

## Scenario

Summarize a completed implementation for the next agent or the user.

## Required behavior

- [ ] Lists changed files and user-visible effects.
- [ ] Records exact validation commands and their outcomes.
- [ ] States residual risks and open questions.
- [ ] Records decisions and rationale that affect future work.
- [ ] Includes a `Do not assume` note when a convention remains unverified.

## Prohibited behavior

- [ ] Does not claim unrun validation succeeded.
- [ ] Does not present assumptions as verified facts.
